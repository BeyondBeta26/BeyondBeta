Who writes Beyond Beta

Our editorial team

Beyond Beta is an independent, temporary research project, which publishes a part of its results in weekly briefings. A small editorial team plus a rotating set of guest contributors is behind these Beyond Beta Briefings. We bring together business familiarity, startup and investor-perspectives, political and policy experience.

For us, tech sovereignty means agency and freedom of choice, not autarchy.

Besides, we want to give a forum to practitioners, business leaders, and researchers working directly on technology sovereignty across Europe and the world to publish their thinking, to connect with each other, and to debate current affairs as well as long-term trends in this fast-moving topic.

If this sounds like you, please get in touch!
