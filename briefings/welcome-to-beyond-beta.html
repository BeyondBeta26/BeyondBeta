Layer 7 · (European) system of laws and values

Welcome to Beyond Beta

2 min read7 Sept 2026

This is the first Briefing on Beyond Beta, and it reads a little differently from the ones that will follow. Instead of analysing a specific development in tech sovereignty, it explains why this project exists at all — and why the page you're on won't stay this quiet for long.

Why we're doing this

Debates about tech sovereignty are currently often confined to the policy bubble, and too seldom connect the political ambition of sovereignty with the practical, multi-national, shareholder-focused perspective of the CEOs and CIOs who actually make the underlying decisions. This disconnect shows up especially in the EU, but also in the Middle East and other parts of the world where sovereignty is hotly debated — including Asia.

Policy initiatives like the Eurostack Initiative sketch a policy- and regulatory-driven path toward sovereignty, and that's important, commendable work. But sovereignty becomes real, in practice, through decisions businesses make about which suppliers, platforms, and technologies they can and can't rely on. Beyond Beta exists to explore that side of it: supply and demand along the tech stack, and the innovators, investors, and policymakers moving it forward.

How it's organised

Every Briefing and Signal from here on will be mapped to one of eight layers of the Sovereignty Stack, from raw materials up to the laws and values that govern how all of it gets used. Briefings run to roughly 500 words and publish weekly; Signals are shorter — built to be read, and shared, in under a minute.

What "sovereignty" means to us

For us, tech sovereignty means agency and freedom of choice, not autarchy. It isn't about disconnecting from the rest of the world — it's about having real options.

What's next

This piece exists so the site doesn't launch to an empty page, and so nobody mistakes a placeholder for a real analysis. From here, weekly Briefings begin in earnest — each one looking at a specific, current development through the lens of one layer of the stack.

If you want to follow along, subscribe below. And if you're a practitioner, researcher, or business leader working directly on technology sovereignty yourself, we want to hear from you.
